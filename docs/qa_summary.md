# 0-shot QA across the four models

Generated by `scripts/report_qa_summary.py`. Four multiple-choice sets — `arc_challenge`, `arc_easy`, `winogrande`, `openbookqa` — scored by likelihood with nothing generated, `acc` throughout (winogrande has no `acc_norm`). Every number is the mean of the four; the per-task columns, the expert counts measured under QA, and the standard errors are in each model's own `results/<model>/RESULTS.md`.

Cells are the QA mean, with the gap to the unpruned model in brackets. The thresholds behind them are not the ones in the generative tables: a threshold admits however many experts the router's confidence lets it, and the router is more decided about a one-sentence question than about the middle of a derivation, so the generative settings spend up to 1.15 fewer experts here than the tier they were calibrated for. Each method was re-solved against these prompts, and the row shown per tier is the cheapest one that still spends at least what Fixed-K does.

Read the two columns as answers to different questions rather than as one method doing better than another.
